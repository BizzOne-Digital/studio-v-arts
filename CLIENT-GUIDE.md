# STUDIO VM Website – Owner's Guide

Welcome to your new website. This guide shows you how to manage everything yourself, with no technical knowledge needed.

**Your admin area:** your website address followed by `/admin` (for example `www.yourwebsite.com/admin`)
**Sign in** with the email and password that BizzOne Digital gave you. Please change the password after your first sign-in and keep it private.

## 2. Adding instructors and classes (step by step)

Set things up in this order: **Instructor → Class → Session.**

### Step 1: Add an instructor (menu: Team)
1. Open **Team** and fill in **Add a team member**.
2. Enter the **Name** (for example, Aisha Khan) and **Role** (for example, Yoga Instructor).
3. Write a short **Biography**. Only include qualifications the person has confirmed.
4. Upload a **Photo** and add a short description of the photo (for example, "Aisha smiling in the studio").
5. Set **Status** to **Published** so the profile shows on the website. "Draft" keeps it hidden.
6. Click **Add team member**.

To change a profile later, click the person's name in the list, edit, and click **Save**. Use **Move up / Move down** to change the order on the website.

### Step 2: Add a class (menu: Services)
A "class" is the activity itself (for example, Yoga, Zumba or Chess). It is created once and then scheduled on many dates.
1. Open **Services** and fill in **Add a service**.
2. **Title:** the class name.
3. **Category:** Move, Create, Learn or Connect.
4. **Booking type:** choose **Class (scheduled sessions)** so people can book dates online.
   - *Studio rental* is for hiring the room.
   - *Enquiry only* shows a "contact us" button instead of online booking.
5. Add a short summary and a description. Add who it is for and the age information if relevant.
6. **Price per person (CAD)**, **Duration** and **Capacity** (number of seats). A class needs a price, either here or on each session, to be bookable online.
7. **Instructor:** click the field and choose a name from your Team list, or type the name of a guest instructor.
8. Upload an **image** and add a short description of it.
9. Set **Status** to **Published** and click **Create service**.

Every service has its own photo. If you upload a new one, the old one is replaced.

### Step 3: Add class dates (menu: Schedule & availability)
1. Open **Schedule & availability** and find **Add class session**.
2. Choose the **Class**, then the **Date**, **Start time** and **Length (minutes)**.
3. Enter the **Capacity (seats)**. Enter a **Price** only if this date should cost something different from the class price.
4. **Instructor:** leave it empty to use the class's instructor, or choose someone else for this date.
5. Set **Status** to **Published** and click **Add session**.

The class now appears on the **Instructors & Classes** page, on the **Schedule** page, and in the booking calendar. Each instructor's profile also shows the classes they teach.

**Good to know**
- The studio is one shared space. If a new session overlaps with another class or a rental, the system will tell you ("That time clashes with another class") so double-booking cannot happen.
- To cancel a session, use **Cancel** next to it. Anyone who booked it is marked cancelled and notified.
- To edit a session, click **Edit** next to it in the upcoming sessions list.

## 3. Managing bookings (menu: Bookings)

1. A customer books online and receives a confirmation that the request is **Pending**.
2. They send the payment by **Interac e-transfer** and quote their reference number.
3. When you have received the payment, open the booking and click **Confirm**. The customer receives a confirmation email.
4. After the session, you can mark it **Completed**.
5. You can **Reschedule** or **Cancel** a booking at any time. The customer is notified.

Click any date on the calendar in **Schedule & availability** or **Bookings** to see everything booked on that day.

---

## 4. Rooms, hours and blocked dates

- **Rooms:** add or edit the spaces people can book, with a photo, capacity and minimum hours. Each room has its own calendar, so a booking in one room does not block another.
- **Opening hours** (Schedule & availability): choose which days are open and the open and close times for rentals.
- **Blocked dates:** add holidays or days you are away. Nothing can be booked on those dates.

## 5. Pricing & offers

- Set the **hourly rental rate**, the **minimum rate** and **tax**.
- Create **offers** (for example, a launch rate or a discount for six or more sessions). Offers never stack: the customer always gets the best single price.

## 6. Gallery, testimonials and FAQs

- **Gallery:** upload new photos with a short description, or remove old ones.
- **Testimonials:** add customer reviews. Mark them **Published** to show them.
- **FAQs:** add a question and answer. You can choose to show some on the home page.

## 7. Website settings

- **Business and contact:** phone, email and business name.
- **Address and map:** where the studio is.
- **Social links:** Instagram and others.
- **Payment and email:** the Interac e-transfer address and the instructions customers receive.
- **Booking terms and waiver:** the rules shown to customers when they book.
- **Go live with online booking:** the switch that opens or closes online booking. Keep it on only when you are ready to take bookings.

---

## 8. Photo tips

- Use bright, sharp photos, ideally wider than they are tall (landscape).
- Any size up to 8 MB is fine. The website shrinks them automatically so pages stay fast.
- Always describe the photo in the "alt text" box. This helps people using screen readers and helps Google.

## 9. Quick answers

**A class is not showing on the website.** Check that the class has **Status: Published**, and that it has at least one **published session** in the future.

**People cannot book a class online.** Make sure the class or session has a **price** and **seats left**. Without a price, visitors see "Enquire" instead of "Book".

**An instructor is not showing.** Open **Team** and check that **Status** is **Published**.

**I changed something but do not see it.** Wait up to 5 minutes and refresh the page (Ctrl+F5).

**I forgot my password.** Contact BizzOne Digital and we will reset it for you.
